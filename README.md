# Portfolio — Sakib Mahmud Sovon

Academic portfolio for Sakib Mahmud Sovon — Computer Vision & Multimodal AI for Accessibility.

**Live:** [sakib-bin-mahmud.github.io/my-academic-portfolio](https://sakib-bin-mahmud.github.io/my-academic-portfolio/) · [my-academic-portfolio-theta.vercel.app](https://my-academic-portfolio-theta.vercel.app/)

## About

Sakib is a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from Khulna University working at the
intersection of computer vision, multimodal AI, and accessibility technology. His research
began with real-time Bengali Sign Language translation — a two-stage YOLOv8n + MobileNetV2
pipeline reaching 99.56% accuracy at a 17.55MB footprint, published at IEEE COMPAS 2025 —
and has since extended to multilingual LLM reasoning. He is currently preparing PhD
applications for Fall 2027 in Computer Vision and Multimodal AI.

This site brings together his research, publications, projects, and teaching and industry
experience in one place.

## What's on the site

- **Research & publications** — IEEE COMPAS 2025, ICSASD 2026, and IEEE CS BDC Symposium 2024,
  including an architecture comparison chart sourced directly from the COMPAS 2025 paper.
- **Projects** — from the BdSL translation pipeline to production backend systems and a
  national government learning platform, each with its own detail page.
- **Teaching & leadership** — four years leading a mathematics outreach program that reached
  5,000+ students across 20 schools.
- **CV download**, and contact / social links (email, GitHub, LinkedIn, Google Scholar, ORCID).

## Design

A **detection-box / annotation aesthetic** — corner brackets, confidence-label tags — drawn
from Sakib's own gesture-detection research rather than a generic template. Deep indigo-navy
base, cyan "detection" accent, warm vermillion used sparingly for awards and honors. Set in
Space Grotesk (display), Source Serif 4 (body), and JetBrains Mono (labels/data).

## Built with

Next.js 14 (App Router), TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS. Deployed to Vercel and GitHub Pages.
